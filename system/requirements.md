# Shared requirements

Adopted from William's 2026-11-07 conversation. These are project instructions,
not replacements for the host's rules, tool permissions, or current user request.

- Preserve the two learning levels: evidence for the AI, readable explanations
  for William. Voice and background research are a separate division of work.
- Keep tools, agent roles, individual workers, and exact models distinct. A
  model choice never grants additional access or changes the evidence standard.
- Share accepted project facts and decisions. Keep working hypotheses and
  model-specific observations visibly scoped, dated, and linked to their basis.
- Use Markdown for authored knowledge, alongside diagram sources, templates,
  styles, code, and small required assets. Generate HTML/PDF reading outputs
  locally where a complete, tested build exists. Preserve existing unique pages.
- Keep large originals separate from the versioned knowledge layer. Record their
  identity and retrieval location; ignored files need their own backup.
- Keep modules independently addressable. Reading another module does not grant
  permission to edit it. Quant integration remains a separate module boundary.
- Retrieve context progressively. Index changes with code; do not ask an AI to
  reread the whole library for each question.
- Before deleting anything, distinguish reproducible output, exact duplicate,
  superseded material, and unique evidence. Unread or unlinked is not disposable.
- Do not commit, create a remote, publish, or deploy merely because the scaffold
  exists. Follow the authorization in the active task.
