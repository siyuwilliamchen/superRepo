---
name: law-boundary-research
description: Researches how one rule or legal regime works in practice and writes a Level 1 card. The rule may be a statute section, an agency test, an exchange rule, a tax provision, or one jurisdiction's tool. The card covers what the rule does by effect (whom it concerns, whose money, whose power) and the gain from following it against the punishment for breaking it, told as a plain story and then explained technically. It covers every lawful way players work around it, searched as widely as the evidence allows (cases, orders, staff and comment letters, filings, practitioner notes, news, forums), with named examples and the pattern across them. Where players stop short, it covers who enforces, the penalties, real enforcement counts and the appeal route. It ends with the true boundary table and quick-fire glossary rows for child pages. Use when a page names a rule whose practical boundary is unknown. One rule or tightly linked group per dispatch; at most 5 per wave.
tools: Read, Grep, Glob, Bash, PowerShell, Write, Edit, WebFetch, WebSearch
